03 / Aerospace · Personal project

UAV frame design

Exploring True-X, H-Frame, and Hybrid-X geometries for a defined payload and mission. Project layout is complete, with CAD development underway.

RolePersonal project · Work in progress
TimelineAug 2026 – Present
Tools & methodsSolidWorks · Planned ANSYS analysis

The objective

Compare True-X, H-Frame, and Hybrid-X drone structures for a defined payload and mission profile, with stiffness-to-weight ratio as a central design criterion.

Progress so far

The project layout is complete and CAD development has begun. The current focus is translating the layout into frame geometry.

Next steps

  • Develop the three candidate frame geometries in SolidWorks.
  • Use ANSYS FEA and CFD to compare structural and aerodynamic behavior.
  • Fabricate and load-test 3D-printed prototypes to compare physical behavior with simulation predictions.

Simulation and physical testing are planned; no performance results are claimed at this stage.
